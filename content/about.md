I'm a hands-on software engineering leader with over a decade of experience building data and AI products. I help engineering teams get real leverage from AI by training them on tools like Claude Code, designing the harnesses that make agents reliable in production, and building custom agents for their workflows. Most recently, I led a health-tech company's rollout of Claude Code: within three months, AI-assisted code made up a large majority of production merges, and the number of PRs the team merged more than doubled.
