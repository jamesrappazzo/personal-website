## Hi, I'm James

I'm an engineering leader focused on building agentic workflows and AI systems. I've spent the last 10+ years working on data and AI products, most recently leading the development of agentic quantitative market research tools.

Currently, I'm doing contracting work building agentic workflows and exploring how AI agents can solve complex problems at scale.
